AnimaPage's own source code is MIT licensed, Copyright (c) 2026 G. De Martino (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The repository also ships

the way each licence was checked are in the linked documents under `docs/`.

## Example lessons and other third-party content

The MIT licence covers AnimaPage's code and the project's own original material only. The lessons
(scenes) under `fixtures/public/scenes/` are of three kinds:

- **Written for the project.** The four example lessons `esempio-*` (the rivers of Europe, the short
  invented story «La torta volante», the water cycle, the noun), the storybook lessons (`storie-trenino`,
  `_dev-storie`, original stories), the façade lessons (`facciata-*`, `_dev-facciata*`), `palcoscenico-dialogo`
  and `cielo-stagioni` (written from scratch for the project), and the scenes
  whose Sources list says "scena di riferimento". The `gen-*` lessons were generated by the pipeline from
  synthetic pages written for the tests: the page numbers they cite ("Pagina del libro, p. …") are invented.
- **Built from public sources.** For example `arte-ultima-cena`, `troia`, `timeline-esempio` and
  `card-esempio`: facts from Wikipedia and Wikidata, told in our own words and cited in each lesson's
  Sources list. The only artwork image shipped is in the public domain (see "Artwork" below).
- **Built while studying real book pages.** Some reference lessons (for example `dati-campania` and
  `matematica-parabola`) are reconstructed from images, books and notes whose
  copyright remains with their respective authors. Their texts are paraphrased, facts and numbers are
  checked against public sources, the book is cited as a source, and no page image is included.

The material that came from protected book pages during development (the page photos, the recorded model
answers that transcribe them, and the lessons generated from them) is not in the public repository.

If you hold rights on something here and want it changed or removed, please open an issue.

## Fonts

No font files are tracked. The app uses the device's system fonts. The exported lessons embed the
KaTeX web fonts (woff2): Copyright (c) 2009-2010 Design Science, Inc. and (c) 2014-2018 Khan Academy,
licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. The prototypes under `docs/reference/prototypes/` load
web fonts from Google Fonts (open-licence families) and three.js from a CDN at view time; nothing is bundled.

## Exported lessons (.html and .wgt)

An exported lesson is one file that carries its own player. Besides AnimaPage's code (MIT) it contains
three.js (MIT, Copyright (c) 2010-2026 Three.js Authors), KaTeX (MIT, Copyright (c) 2013-2020 Khan Academy
and other contributors) with its fonts (SIL OFL 1.1, see "Fonts"), zod (MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Colin
McDonnell), and the assets the lesson uses: CC0 models and sounds, public-domain images, and geodata with
the attributions above. These code notices, with the MIT licence text, are written in a `<!-- licences -->`
comment at the top of every exported file (`apps/web/src/export/assemble.ts`, `LICENCE_NOTICES`). The lesson's
**Sources** list (in the app and in the exported file) names every source with its licence: keep it
when you share a lesson. A lesson made from a photographed book page also carries that page's content:
see the README, "Legal and privacy".
